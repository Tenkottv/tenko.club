Privacy notice

What we collect

The only personal information Tenko Club collects is the email address you type into the club sign-up form. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this website.

Why we collect it

We use your email address to tell you about new drops before they go on general release. We only email you because you asked us to, so our lawful basis under UK data protection law is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time.

Who handles it for us

  • Kit stores the club mailing list and sends our emails. Kit is based in the United States. Its privacy policy explains how it protects data it holds for us.
  • GitHub hosts this website. Like most web hosts, it records visitors' IP addresses for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

We never sell or share your email address with anyone else.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe. After that, it is removed from our list.

Your rights

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. You can also reply to any of our emails to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
